## 3. List of Agreements Reached

- **Meeting Schedule & Cadence**:
  - Agreed to adjust meeting frequency from bi-weekly to monthly meetings held on the first Wednesday of each month at 10:00 AM Eastern / 16:00 CEST (2025-08-05, 2025-08-19, 2025-12-03).
  - Agreed to update the official W3C calendar with the new monthly event link (2025-12-03).

- **Task Force Scope & Boundaries**:
  - Agreed that the task force focuses strictly on protocol extensions and technical specifications supporting trust and safety, not on platform-specific moderation policies or AI/ML moderation algorithms (2025-01-07, 2025-01-21).
  - Agreed to maintain an explicit "out of scope" document via issue [#47](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/47) to keep task force discussions focused (2025-01-07).
  - Agreed to close issue [#19](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/19) (FediBlock / domain blocking specification) as "not planned" for core protocol specification (2025-05-27).
  - Agreed to defer quote post safety definitions to Mastodon's work on [FEP-044f](https://codeberg.org/fediverse/fep/src/branch/main/fep/044f/fep-044f.md) (2025-01-07, 2025-05-27).

- **Initial Report Framing & Baseline Requirements**:
  - Agreed on the structure of the Initial Report via PR [#51](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/pull/51) to serve as a non-normative framing document bridging formal specs, existing implementations, and future workstreams (2025-01-21, 2025-03-04).
  - Agreed that bare minimum requirements for fediverse software implementations include server-level domain blocking (both inbound and outbound) and report handling capabilities (sending/receiving reports) (2025-01-21).

- **Content Warnings, Labels, and `summary` Semantics**:
  - Agreed that `summary` in AS2/AP is intended for general content descriptions (which can naturally include text warnings) and should return to its original summary semantics rather than being misused as a sole CW flag (2025-05-13, 2025-06-10, 2025-12-03).
  - Agreed that `as:sensitive` represents an author's "viewer discretion advised" indicator to signal UI collapsing, though client rendering rules remain configurable by readers (2025-05-13, 2025-06-10).
  - Agreed to advance a Content Labels specification (FEP-4c96) based on FIRES (`fires:Label`), attaching structured IRI references in the `tag` property of objects to allow granular, machine-readable filtering, blurring, or hiding (2025-05-13, 2025-05-27, 2025-06-10, 2025-12-03).
  - Agreed to close Issue #1 as "not planned" for a new standalone CW property/FEP, concluding that `summary` combined with `as:sensitive` and structured Content Labels provides a superior solution (2025-12-03).
  - Agreed that Content Labels are specifically designed for trust, safety, and moderation purposes, distinct from decorative "badges" or taxonomical "flairs" (2025-12-03).

- **NLNet Grant Application & Funding**:
  - Agreed to seek external funding from the NLNet Commons Fund to support task force spec writing, meeting administration, and professional scribe services (2025-08-05, 2025-08-19, 2025-12-03).
  - Agreed that co-chairs Emelia Smith and Darius Kazemi coordinate and administer the EUR 25,000 NLNet grant application on behalf of the task force (2025-08-19, 2025-12-03).
