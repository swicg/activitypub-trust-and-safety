## 3. List of Agreements Reached

- **Content Warning Approach (No New CW Property)**:
  - Agreed not to define a dedicated new `content_warning` property in ActivityPub (which would introduce migration issues and confusion with `summary`), but instead to document and rely on the existing convention of `summary` + `as:sensitive` (**RESOLVED**, 2026-01-07, 2026-05-06, 2026-06-17, 2026-08-05).
- **Creative Commons Signals Issue Closure**:
  - Agreed to close Creative Commons Signals issue ([#96](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/96)) as "won't fix" due to community feedback regarding opt-out vs opt-in consent assumptions (**RESOLVED**, 2026-01-07).
- **Domain Extension of `as:sensitive`**:
  - Confirmed and agreed on the expanded domain of `as:sensitive` to apply to `Link` objects (e.g. as attachments) as well as `Object` types, documented in the SWICG miscellany specification (**RESOLVED**, 2026-01-07, 2026-05-06, 2026-06-17).
- **Issue Closures (Peer Moderation & SWICG Calendar)**:
  - Agreed to close the Peer Moderation issue due to lack of demand/implementation precedent, noting it requires moderation actors ([#24](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/24)) (**RESOLVED**, 2026-02-04).
  - Agreed to close the SWICG Calendar issue, untagging it as editorial to ensure it posts to the mailing list (**RESOLVED**, 2026-02-04).
- **Moderator Discovery & Addressing Scope (`moderatedBy`)**:
  - Agreed that moderation addressing should focus narrowly on a direct `moderatedBy` relationship pointing to a moderation actor/inbox, avoiding introducing broad "host" or "server" concepts into ActivityPub vocabulary (**RESOLVED**, 2026-05-06, 2026-06-17).
  - Agreed that self-asserted `moderatedBy` relationships must be verifiable to prevent malicious misattribution (e.g., `evil.com` claiming to be moderated by `mastodon.social`), leaning towards authorization stamps or response-level HTTP Message Signatures (**RESOLVED**, 2026-05-06, 2026-06-17).
- **Content Labeling Architecture**:
  - Agreed that Content Labeling ([#84](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/84) / PR [#116](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/pull/116)) will distinguish first-party (self-applied) labels from third-party / moderator-applied labels. First-party labels are placed in the `tag` array using `Label` objects referencing canonical `LabelDefinition` URIs via `href` (**RESOLVED**, 2026-05-06, 2026-06-17).
  - Agreed not to mandate a single canonical label vocabulary within the core specification report to avoid bikeshedding, leaving vocabulary definitions to implementer consensus (**RESOLVED**, 2026-06-17).
- **Initial Report Sunset & Workstream Restructuring**:
  - Agreed to abandon the monolithic "Initial Report" framing document in favor of separate, modular, specification-track documents, FEPs, and explainer reports, rehoming remaining initial report issues ([#32](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/32)) into distinct workstreams (**RESOLVED**, 2026-08-05).
- **12-Month Workstream Approvals (NLNet Grant)**:
  - Agreed on approved workstreams for the upcoming 12-month NLNet grant (**RESOLVED**, 2026-08-19):
    1. *Interaction Controls, Replies, and Quote Posts*: Produce a specification-track document (Champion: Julian Fietkau, with help from `a`; Discussion [#152](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/discussions/152)).
    2. *Best Practices for ActivityPub Implementors*: Proactive recommendations covering untrusted remote data, date handling, moderation types, open registrations, and federation management (Champions: `a` & Roel Roscam Abbing; Issues [#29](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/29), [#36](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/36), [#33](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/33), [#68](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/68), [#67](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/67)).
    3. *Moderation Actions*: Specifications for suspensions and hiding content under moderation (Champion: `a`; Issues [#95](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/95), [#64](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/64)).
    4. *Improvements to Moderation Activities & UX*: Formalizing `Flag` activities, S2S `Block` handling, and reporting UX (Issues [#14](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/14), [#8](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/8), [#3](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/3), [#2](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/2), [#23](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/23), [#71](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/71), [#69](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/69)).
  - Rejected adding automated filtering / anti-spam explainer ([#28](https://github.com/swicg/activitypub-trust-and-safety/issues/28)) to the 12-month grant workstreams (**RESOLVED**, 2026-08-19).
